    How to play

    Survival

    Waves of monsters pour into the arena. Clear a wave to pick an upgrade. Every fifth wave, the Hollow King shows up.

    When a teammate falls, stand next to them to revive them. If everyone is down, the run ends.

    Versus

    Everyone for themselves. First to 7 kills wins, or whoever leads when the 3-minute clock runs out.
