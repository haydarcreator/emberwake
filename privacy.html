The short version

  • You can play Emberwake without an account. Without one, we don’t ask for any personal data.
  • If you sign in, we keep your name, email address and sign-in method.
  • Your game stats are stored in your own browser.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, and we never sell data.
  • You can delete your account at any time from your profile page.

1. Who we are

Emberwake (“we”, “us”) operates this website and the Emberwake game. We are the controller of the personal data described here. This policy covers the website, the game, accounts, and the multiplayer servers we run.

2. What we collect

If you create an account or sign in

  • Email and password: your name, email address and password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and stored only as a salted hash. We never see them.
  • Google sign-in: the name, email address, profile photo (if any) and account identifier that Google shares after you approve it.
  • Account metadata: when your account was created and when you last signed in.

When you play

  • Game stats (games played, best wave, high score, kills, versus wins, time played) and settings (volume, controls) are saved in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to us.
  • Online and LAN games: see Multiplayer below.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider records standard request data such as IP address, time, requested page and browser type, for security and reliability.

We don’t collect payment details, precise location, contacts, voice or chat messages, or any special categories of personal data.

3. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To show your name to other players in multiplayer games.
  • To send emails you ask for, such as email verification and password resets.
  • To detect and prevent cheating, abuse and attacks.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don’t use your data for advertising, profiling or automated decisions with legal or similar effects.

5. Multiplayer

When you play online or on a LAN, your device connects to an Emberwake game server. The server receives your display name, your game inputs (movement, aiming, shooting) and your IP address, which it needs to run the match. It shares your display name and in-game actions with the other players in your room. Match data is kept in memory only and is discarded when the room closes; we do not store match histories or IP addresses from game servers.

LAN games run on a server that one of the players hosts on their own computer. That player, not us, controls that server.

Pick a display name that doesn’t reveal more than you want other players to see.

6. Service providers

We share personal data only with providers that help us run the service, under contracts that limit how they use it:

  • Google Firebase Authentication (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.): stores account records and handles sign-in.
  • Google Sign-In: only if you choose to sign in with Google. Google’s privacy policy applies to the data it holds.
  • Google Fonts: our pages load fonts from Google’s servers, which receive your IP address when the fonts are requested.
  • Our hosting provider: serves the website and runs the online game server.

We may disclose data if the law requires it, to protect the rights and safety of players or the public, or as part of a transfer of the game, in which case this policy continues to apply. We do not sell or rent personal data.

8. Retention

  • Account data: while your account is active, and deleted within 30 days after you delete it.
  • Server logs: up to 90 days, longer only if needed to investigate a security incident.
  • Multiplayer match data: only for the length of the match.
  • Data in your browser: until you clear it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, to receive it in a portable format, and to complain to your data protection authority. You can delete your account yourself on your profile page.

California residents have the rights to know, delete and correct personal information and not to be discriminated against for using them. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

To make a request, email us at the address below. We reply within 30 days and may ask you to confirm your identity.

10. Security

Traffic to the site and online game servers is encrypted with HTTPS and secure WebSockets. Passwords are never stored in plain text, and access to account data is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. When data leaves the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, we rely on adequacy decisions (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified) or Standard Contractual Clauses.

12. Children

Accounts are for players aged 13 and over (16 in some EU countries, unless a parent consents). Anyone can play without an account. If you believe a child under the applicable age created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we’ll also notify account holders by email or with a notice on the site before they take effect.
