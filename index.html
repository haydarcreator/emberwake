What you’re in for

Short rounds, quick to learn, hard to put down.

🔥

Endless waves

Slimes, bats, skeleton archers and charging brutes. Every wave is bigger and meaner than the last.

👑

The Hollow King

Every fifth wave the boss arrives, filling the arena with bullets and calling in reinforcements. It gets angrier at half health.

✨

Build your mage

Pick one of three upgrades after each wave: split shot, piercing bolts, faster casting, regeneration and more.

🤝

Real co-op

When a friend goes down, stand next to them to bring them back. The run only ends when the whole team falls.

⚔️

Versus mode

Turn on each other. First to seven kills, or whoever leads when the three-minute clock runs out.

🎮

Play your way

Mouse aiming, auto-aim on the keyboard, or twin-stick on a gamepad. Up to four controllers on one screen.

Download Emberwake

Free on Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. We picked the right file for your device.

Your computer Download Emberwake

All platforms

  1. Download the .msi (or the .exe setup) and open it.
  2. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. This appears for new games that aren’t code-signed yet.
  3. Follow the installer. Emberwake gets a Start menu entry and a desktop shortcut.
  4. The first time you host a LAN game, allow Emberwake through the Windows firewall on private networks.

To uninstall: Settings → Apps → Installed apps → Emberwake.

  1. Download the .dmg for your Mac: Apple silicon (M1 and later) or Intel. Not sure? Apple menu → About This Mac.
  2. Open the .dmg and drag Emberwake into Applications.
  3. The first time, right-click Emberwake in Applications and choose Open, then Open again. After that it opens normally.
  1. Any distribution: download the .AppImage, make it executable and run it:
chmod +x Emberwake-*.AppImage && ./Emberwake-*.AppImage
  1. Ubuntu, Debian, Mint: download the .deb and install it:
sudo apt install ./emberwake_*_amd64.deb
  1. Download the .apk on your phone or tablet.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install. Play with the on-screen sticks or a Bluetooth gamepad.

Apple only allows apps on iPhone and iPad through the App Store, so there’s no file to download. You can still install Emberwake from Safari:

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open Emberwake from your home screen. It runs full-screen with touch controls.

Don’t want to install anything? Play in your browser. You can also (tiny, updates itself).

Host a LAN party

Playing on the same Wi-Fi needs the small Emberwake server on one computer. It needs Node.js 18 or newer and nothing else.

  1. Download the game (.zip, about 100 KB), unzip it and open a terminal in the folder.
  2. Run the server:
node server.js
  1. It prints an address like http://192.168.1.20:8080. Everyone opens it, picks Online & LAN, and joins your room code.

Questions

Is Emberwake free?

Yes. No ads, no purchases, no account required.

Why should I create an account?

An account keeps your best wave, score and wins, and uses your name in online games. You can sign in with Google or with an email and password.

Windows says “Windows protected your PC”. Is it safe?

Windows shows this for new apps that haven’t built up a reputation or been code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. Every installer on this page is built automatically from the game’s source code.

Is there an iPhone app?

Apple only allows App Store apps on iPhone and iPad. Until Emberwake is on the App Store, open this page in Safari and use Share → Add to Home Screen. You get a full-screen game with touch controls.

Can I play offline?

Once installed, solo and local multiplayer work without a connection. Online and LAN play need the game server.

Which gamepads work?

Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro and most USB or Bluetooth controllers that your browser recognises as a standard gamepad.
